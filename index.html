PART 5 Questions 33–40

Transformations

Complete the second sentence so that it means the same as the first sentence, using the word given. Do not change the word given. You must use between two and five words, including the word given.

33. Pat no longer skis, does he?

ANYMORE

Pat does he?

34. In the past, I enjoyed sailing but I don't anymore.

USED

I sailing but I don't anymore.

35. In the past, companies only gave their employees three or four days' holiday each year.

WOULD

In the past, companies their employees three or four days' holiday each year.

36. It took Mary a long time to feel comfortable speaking Spanish.

USED

It took Mary a long time speaking Spanish.

37. Everyone should show their ID when they arrive.

SUPPOSED

Everyone show their ID when they arrive.

38. Paul was forced by his wife to give up smoking.

MADE

Paul's wife smoking.

39. My mum says it's OK for me to have her old computer once she replaces it.

Once she gets a new computer, my mum her old one.

40. You mustn't eat in the library.
